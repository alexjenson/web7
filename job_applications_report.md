faster"), followed immediately by a pricing anchor and a single sign-up button. Detailed
features moved to a secondary fold for users who needed more convincing.

viewport. I also compressed the lead form from 6 fields to 3. Separately, I increased the
contrast ratio of the primary CTA button after noticing it was visually competing with a
